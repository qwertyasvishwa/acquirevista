This page is intentionally implemented as a draft because the client’s legal identity, data controller details, and approved contact routes were not provided. Replace the sections below before any production launch.

What this site is expected to collect

The site is designed to collect consultation enquiries and sample lead requests. That can include name, work email, company name, optional phone or WhatsApp details, service interest, monthly lead goals, and free-text messages.

How the information is intended to be used

  • Route new business enquiries to the AcquireVista sales process.
  • Review service fit before booking a consultation or sharing sample lead material.
  • Respond to direct contact requests submitted through the site.

What still needs to be added before launch

  • Legal business name and jurisdiction
  • Official privacy contact or DPO contact details
  • Exact retention period and lawful basis language
  • Third-party processors used for form handling, CRM routing, analytics, or messaging
  • Cookie and consent handling language if tracking expands in production

Interim operational note

Until the live endpoint, analytics stack, and business identity are confirmed, this build should be treated as a preview implementation rather than a launch-ready legal document set.
