Privacy

A straightforward policy for a straightforward contact experience.

This policy explains the information AcquireVista may receive when you contact us and how it is used.

Last updated: July 2026

Who we are

AcquireVista provides qualification-first outbound growth services for B2B revenue teams, agencies, and consultants. You can contact us at hello@acquirevista.com.

Information you choose to share

If you use a contact link or email us, we may receive the information included in your message, such as your name, work email, company, phone number, business context, and service interest.

How we use it

We use contact information only to respond to enquiries, assess a potential engagement, communicate about requested services, and maintain appropriate business records.

Website forms

This website currently prepares a message in your email application. It does not transmit form entries to an AcquireVista server. Your email provider’s own privacy terms apply once you send an email.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell personal information. We may use service providers that support normal business operations where necessary. We retain information only for as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described above or to meet legal obligations.

Your choices

You may ask about, correct, or request deletion of personal information we hold by emailing us. We will respond in line with applicable law.

Policy updates

We may update this policy when our practices or legal obligations change. The latest version will be available on this page.
